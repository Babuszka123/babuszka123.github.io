Jchess: free online chess

Play chess with friends on your phone or computer. Get a quick match with a random opponent, join an open game, or send a private code to a friend. You can also play a bot (five levels, from beginner to master) or play two-player on one screen.

After each game you can review it with Stockfish: best moves, move ratings and your own lines. There is a chess clock, board and piece themes, and you can draw arrows on the board. No account needed. There is also an Android app.
